From ancient redwoods to coastal bluffs — Santa Cruz outdoor options are genuinely world-class.

🌊 West Cliff Walk
Iconic coastal bluff path. Dolphins, surfers, and the best sunset views in town.
🌲 Henry Cowell Redwoods
Ancient giants on a flat, stroller-friendly grove loop. Easy for all ages.
🏖️ Wilder Ranch — Ocean Walk
Coastal trail with sweeping ocean views. Farmhouse ruins and wildflowers too.
🧚 Enchanted Forest Trail
A genuinely magical forest trail — the name doesn't lie.
🌿 Nisene Marks State Park
Deep forest hiking in the Santa Cruz Mountains — miles of quiet trails.
🛐 Land of Medicine Buddha
Forest trails and meditation classes. A genuinely peaceful escape.
🌸 UCSC Arboretum & Botanical Garden
Outstanding birding. Rare plant collections. Stunning hillside campus setting.
Great for Birding
🚶 Santa Cruz Historical Walks
Local guided tours — 3 routes to choose from. History, architecture, great stories.
🏄 Surf Lessons at Cowell Beach
Beginner-friendly surf school right at Cowell Beach — the most protected, mellow break in town, right next to the Boardwalk. Best spot to learn in Santa Cruz.
Cowell BeachBook Ahead
🌊 Richard Schmidt Surf School
One of the most respected surf schools in Santa Cruz — Richard has been teaching here for decades. A local institution.
Local InstitutionBook Ahead

Santa Cruz punches way above its weight musically. Jazz, indie, Latin, Afrobeat — all within a few miles of each other.

World-class jazz in an intimate 200-seat room. One of the best small jazz venues in the country. 320 Cedar St, downtown.
JazzDowntown

The big room. National touring acts, local legends, and everything in between since 1969. 1011 Pacific Ave.

OutdoorUCSC

DanceFreeWharf

SUP yoga in the harbor, sunrise flows on the beach, float tanks, redwood retreats — Santa Cruz is a wellness town through and through.

🏄 SUP Yoga at the Harbor
Yoga on paddleboards in the calm harbor waters. Saturday & Sunday at 8:30 AM. $45/person. All levels welcome.
SUP YogaHarbor
🌅 Sunrise Yoga at La Bahia
All-levels yoga flow on the seaside roof deck at La Bahia Hotel. Saturday & Sunday 9 AM. Mats provided, space limited.
YogaOceanfront
🌲 Sunday Yoga at Trout Farm Inn
Outdoor all-levels yoga among the redwoods in Felton. Wednesdays & Sundays at 9 AM. $25/person.
YogaRedwoodsFelton
💪 Coastline Pilates
Locally female-owned studio at 406 Mission St with 25+ group classes weekly. Reformer and mat classes with small class sizes.
PilatesStudio
🧊 Sage Float Spa
Sensory deprivation float tanks, infrared sauna, cold plunge, and red light therapy. 1395 41st Ave, Capitola.
FloatSpaCapitola
🙏 Land of Medicine Buddha
Tibetan Buddhist retreat on 108 acres in Soquel. Daily classes, yoga, meditation, and hiking trails. 19 private retreat rooms.
RetreatMeditationSoquel
💆 Essentia Body Therapy
Premium massage therapy in a historic Victorian at 556 Ocean View Ave. Swedish, deep tissue, prenatal, sports, and craniosacral.
MassageTherapy
🏔️ 1440 Multiversity
75-acre luxury retreat in the Scotts Valley redwoods. Immersive workshops, world-class spa, farm-to-table dining. Day visitors welcome.
RetreatWellnessScotts Valley
